# Investment Fundamentals

## Core asset classes
- **Cash / deposits**: capital stable, return often below inflation.
- **Sukuk and fixed income**: contractual profit, moderate interest-rate risk.
- **Equities (e.g. KSE-100)**: high expected return, high volatility, need a
  5+ year horizon.
- **Gold**: traditional inflation hedge in South Asia, no cash flow.
- **Property**: illiquid, transaction costs high, leverage common.
- **Crypto**: extremely high volatility; size positions as risk capital only.

## Diversification and risk
Risk is driven less by the number of holdings than by their correlation.
A portfolio of ten highly correlated local equities is not diversified.
Volatility is measured by standard deviation; FinTwin-X stores per-asset
volatility and applies it in the return simulation.

## Time horizon and sequence risk
For goals under 3 years, volatility dominates and capital protection matters.
For goals beyond 10 years, contribution rate matters more than return.
Sequence-of-returns risk — a bad market in the final years before a goal — is
why the engine reports the 5th percentile outcome alongside the mean.

## Costs and taxes
Fees, spreads and taxes compound negatively. Compare net-of-cost, net-of-tax
returns, and prefer liquid instruments for near-term goals.
