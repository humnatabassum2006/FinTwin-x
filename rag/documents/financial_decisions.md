# Big-Ticket Purchase Decisions

## The question to ask
Not "can I make the payment?" but "what does this do to my probability of
reaching the goals I already have, and to my resilience if income drops?"

## Anatomy of a financed purchase
Total cost = down payment + Σ EMI + opportunity cost of the down payment had it
been invested. A Rs 3,000,000 loan at 20% over 5 years costs about Rs 4.75m in
total payments; the vehicle also depreciates roughly 12% per year.

## Decision framework used by FinTwin-X
1. Baseline: simulate the current path (goal probability, stress probability).
2. Decision: add the purchase (down payment + new EMI) and re-simulate.
3. Compare: the delta in goal probability is the true price of the decision.
4. Optimise: search delay / smaller ticket / longer tenor / income boost /
   spending cut, and recommend the least aggressive option that still reaches
   the chosen target probability (default 75%).

## Rules of thumb
- Keep total DSR below 0.36 after the purchase.
- Keep at least 3 months of expenses liquid after the down payment.
- If a purchase drops goal probability below ~50%, delay it.

## Communication standards
Every projection must be presented with its probability, its assumptions and
its data timestamp. Never present a simulated outcome as a certainty.
